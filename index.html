What we're building

VYBE is in active development. These are the experiences on our product roadmap — some are in progress, others are planned for later releases.

  • Social Messaging

    Fast, expressive one-to-one and group conversations with the people who matter.

  • Circles & Communities

    Spaces built around shared interests, where small groups and larger communities can grow.

  • AI Personas

    Playful, opt-in characters you can chat with — designed to spark ideas and conversation.

  • Meme Studio

    Create, remix and share memes with your friends and circles, right inside the app.

  • Caption Battle

    A friendly game: everyone captions the same image and the community picks a favourite.

  • Voice Experiences

    Voice notes and live audio ways to hang out when typing isn't enough.

About VYBE

VYBE is an independent, student-developed social communication and community platform. We're building a place to find your people, express yourself and have fun together. The product hasn't launched yet — this page will be updated as VYBE gets closer to release.
